Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Last updated: 1 September 2026

The short version: Oblivion does not collect anything about you, because there is nowhere for it to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing.

Oblivion has no server. There is no backend service, no database, no account system, and no analytics. We have no way to receive information about you, and we do not want one.

On Android, the app does not hold the INTERNET permission. You can verify that yourself: Settings → Apps → Oblivion → Permissions, or run apkanalyzer manifest permissions against the published package. The app cannot open a network connection, because the operating system will not let it.

Your notes

What you type in Oblivion is held in your device's memory and nowhere else.

It is never written to your device's storage. It is not backed up. It is not synchronised. It is not transmitted anywhere. There is no history, archive, draft, or trash to recover it from.

When you destroy a note, the text is cleared and released before the destruction animation begins — what you watch is an image of your words, not the words themselves. When you leave the app, or switch away from it, the note is erased immediately.

We never see your notes. We never receive their contents, their length, how many you write, or when you write them. We collect no statistics of any kind about them.

Settings

The app stores your preferences — the optional biometric lock, the panic gesture, note typeface, sound, haptics, reduce motion, and the character counter — on your device, using the operating system's standard preference storage.

These are switches and one typeface name. They contain no text you have written. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions

  • Vibration — to play the haptics that accompany each destruction.
  • Biometrics / fingerprint — only if you switch on the optional lock. The

    • check is performed by your device; we never see the result of it beyond "unlocked" or "not unlocked", and no biometric data reaches the app.

That is the complete list.

Payment

Oblivion is a one-time paid app. Your purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, which grants it to your Google account. We never see your name, email, payment details, or any identifier connected to the purchase. The app itself contains no in-app purchases and no billing code.

Children

Oblivion is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. There is nothing to collect.

What we cannot promise

Two honest limits, stated plainly rather than buried:

  • Memory is managed by the operating system. We release your text the

    • moment a note is destroyed, but no app can guarantee that every trace of it is scrubbed from device memory before the system reuses that memory. This is a property of how phones work, not a choice we made.

  • On iOS, screenshots cannot be blocked. On Android we set FLAG_SECURE,

    • which blocks screenshots, screen recording, and the app-switcher preview. iOS provides no equivalent, so there we obscure the app-switcher preview and nothing more.

If someone is holding your unlocked phone, no app can save you.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect it to.
